# Corporate Account Lockout Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard process for handling employee account
lockouts caused by repeated authentication failures.

## Lockout Threshold

An employee account is considered at risk of lockout when repeated
authentication failures occur.

The enterprise identity system tracks failed authentication attempts
for each employee account.

IT support should verify the account status before recommending account
recovery actions.

## Identifying a Locked Account

Employees may experience an account lockout when authentication fails
repeatedly.

IT support can verify account state through the approved account status
system.

A locked account should not be treated as proof that the employee has
forgotten their password.

## Employee Recovery

Employees should use the approved password recovery process when their
account is eligible.

If the account is not eligible for automated recovery, the request must
be handled by IT support according to the account recovery procedure.

Employees must not attempt to bypass enterprise authentication controls.

## Security Requirements

IT support personnel must never request an employee's password.

Employees must not provide passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, or
authentication secrets through support channels.

## Escalation

Escalate the request when:

- The account cannot be located in the enterprise identity system.
- The account state is inconsistent across systems.
- Automated recovery is unavailable.
- There is evidence of suspicious authentication activity.
- The employee cannot complete the approved recovery process.

## Related Procedures

Refer to the approved password recovery and MFA procedures when
additional authentication assistance is required.
