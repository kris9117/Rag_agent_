# Corporate Hardware Support Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines the standard process for reporting hardware
problems with managed employee devices.

## Common Hardware Problems

Examples include:

- Laptop will not power on.
- Display problems.
- Keyboard or trackpad failure.
- Battery problems.
- Docking-station problems.
- External monitor problems.

## Initial Troubleshooting

Confirm that power cables and approved docking connections are secure.

For display problems, verify that the monitor is powered on and that
the correct display connection is selected.

Restart the device when possible.

## Reporting

When contacting IT support, provide the device identifier and a concise
description of the hardware symptoms.

Do not disassemble corporate hardware unless explicitly authorized.

## Escalation

Hardware issues that cannot be resolved through basic troubleshooting
should be escalated to the appropriate IT support team.

Suspected physical damage should be reported rather than repaired by
the employee.
