# Corporate Password Reset Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the approved process for employee password resets.

## Eligibility

Password recovery is available only when the employee account satisfies
the enterprise recovery requirements.

The account must be active and required authentication controls must be
available.

IT support should verify password reset eligibility before initiating
recovery procedures.

## Standard Process

Employees should use the approved password recovery mechanism.

The recovery process may require additional authentication such as MFA.

Employees should not share authentication codes with another person.

## Failed Authentication

Repeated failed authentication attempts may cause an account to become
locked.

Employees should stop repeatedly attempting authentication if they
believe the account may be locked.

IT support should verify the account status before recommending further
attempts.

## Security Restrictions

IT support must never ask for:

- Employee passwords
- MFA codes
- Recovery codes
- Authentication tokens

These credentials must remain confidential.

## Escalation

Escalate the request when:

- Password recovery eligibility cannot be determined.
- The account is not active.
- Required authentication controls are unavailable.
- The account state is inconsistent.
- Suspicious activity is detected.
