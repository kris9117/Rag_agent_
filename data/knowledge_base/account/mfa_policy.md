# Corporate MFA Policy

## Purpose

Multi-factor authentication is required for supported enterprise
accounts and applications.

## MFA Enrollment

Employees must complete MFA enrollment using the enterprise-approved
authentication method.

IT support may verify whether MFA is enabled for an employee account.

## MFA Authentication

Employees should approve only authentication requests that they
personally initiated.

Unexpected MFA prompts should not be approved.

## Lost or Unavailable MFA Device

Employees who lose access to their registered MFA device should contact
IT support and follow the approved recovery process.

IT support must verify the employee identity using approved procedures.

## Security Requirements

Employees must never share MFA codes with support personnel.

Employees should report unexpected MFA prompts because they may indicate
attempted unauthorized access.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- MFA enrollment cannot be verified.
- The registered MFA device is unavailable.
- Repeated unexpected MFA prompts are reported.
- Identity verification cannot be completed.
- Suspicious authentication activity is suspected.
