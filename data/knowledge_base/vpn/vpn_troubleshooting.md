# Corporate VPN Troubleshooting Guide

## Purpose

This guide provides troubleshooting steps for employees who cannot
connect to the corporate VPN.

## Supported VPN Client

The corporate environment supports the approved enterprise VPN client
installed on managed employee devices.

Employees should use the company-provided VPN client rather than
unapproved third-party VPN software.

## Basic Troubleshooting

Before opening an IT support ticket, perform the following checks:

1. Confirm that the device has an active internet connection.
2. Verify that the corporate VPN client is running.
3. Sign out of the VPN client and sign in again.
4. Restart the VPN client if the connection remains unavailable.
5. Restart the device if the VPN client continues to fail.

## VPN Connection Status

If the VPN client reports that authentication failed, verify that the
employee account is active and that required authentication methods are
available.

If the account is locked, the employee should contact IT support or
follow the approved account recovery process.

## Network Troubleshooting

If the VPN client cannot reach the corporate gateway:

- Confirm internet connectivity.
- Try connecting from another approved network if available.
- Check whether the device reports a healthy network connection.
- Avoid repeatedly retrying authentication when the account may be
  locked.

## When to Contact IT Support

Contact IT support when:

- The VPN client continues to fail after basic troubleshooting.
- Authentication repeatedly fails.
- The employee cannot access the approved VPN client.
- The device has connectivity problems that prevent VPN access.
- The employee receives an error that cannot be resolved using this guide.

## Security Restrictions

Employees must not share passwords, MFA codes, recovery codes, or
other authentication secrets with IT support personnel.

IT support will never request an employee's password or MFA code through
the support channel.
