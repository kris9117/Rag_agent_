# Mobile Corporate Email Access

## Purpose

This guide covers approved access to corporate email from managed mobile
devices.

## Supported Access

Employees should use the organization-approved mobile email
configuration.

Personal or unapproved applications should not be used to bypass
enterprise email controls.

## Authentication

Mobile email access may require enterprise authentication and MFA.

Unexpected MFA requests must not be approved.

## Synchronization Problems

Confirm network connectivity and verify that the approved email
configuration is active.

Restart the email application when synchronization stops.

## Security

Lost mobile devices must be reported promptly so appropriate access
controls can be applied.

Employees must not share authentication codes with another person.
