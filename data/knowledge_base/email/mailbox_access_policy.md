# Corporate Mailbox Access Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines access requirements for employee corporate
mailboxes.

## Access Requirements

Mailbox access requires an active employee account and approved
authentication.

Access should be provided only through approved enterprise applications
and services.

## Shared Mailboxes

Access to shared mailboxes must be granted through the organization's
approved access-management process.

Employees should not share personal credentials to provide another
employee with mailbox access.

## Access Problems

If mailbox access is denied, verify the employee account status and
confirm whether the requested mailbox access has been provisioned.

## Security

Employees must not attempt to bypass mailbox permissions.

Suspected unauthorized mailbox access must be reported to IT support or
the appropriate security team.
