# Corporate Email Troubleshooting Guide

## Purpose

This guide covers common problems accessing the corporate email service.

## Basic Checks

Confirm that the device has network connectivity.

Verify that the approved email application is running.

Restart the email application if messages are not loading correctly.

## Authentication Problems

If authentication fails, verify the employee account status using the
approved account-management process.

Do not repeatedly enter credentials if the account may be locked.

Employees must not provide passwords or MFA codes to support personnel.

## Mailbox Synchronization

If recent messages are missing, confirm that the device has network
connectivity and restart the approved email application.

Persistent synchronization problems should be reported to IT support.

## Escalation

Escalate when authentication remains unavailable, mailbox access cannot
be restored, or the issue affects multiple enterprise services.
