# Corporate VPN Authentication Guide

## Purpose

This guide addresses VPN authentication failures.

## Authentication Requirements

VPN access requires an active enterprise account and the organization's
approved authentication mechanisms.

## Authentication Failure

If VPN authentication fails, verify the account status before
repeatedly attempting to authenticate.

An account that is locked may require the approved account recovery
process.

## MFA

If MFA is required, employees should approve only authentication
requests they personally initiated.

Unexpected MFA prompts must not be approved.

## Escalation

Escalate persistent authentication failures when the account is active
but VPN authentication remains unavailable.
