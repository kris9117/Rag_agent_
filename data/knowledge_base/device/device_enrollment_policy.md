# Managed Device Enrollment Policy

## Purpose

Corporate laptops must be enrolled in the approved device-management
system before they are used for enterprise work.

## Enrollment Requirements

The device must be assigned to an employee and registered with the
enterprise device-management service.

Only approved corporate devices may access protected enterprise
resources.

## New Devices

Employees receiving a new corporate device should complete the
organization's standard enrollment process before accessing enterprise
applications.

Do not attempt to bypass device-management controls.

## Enrollment Problems

Contact IT support when:

- The device cannot be enrolled.
- The device is not associated with the expected employee.
- Device-management status appears incorrect.
- Required enterprise applications cannot be installed.

## Security

Employees must not disable enterprise device-management controls.

Unauthorized removal of device-management software may result in
restricted access to corporate resources.
